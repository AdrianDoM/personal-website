A continuous map of metric spaces whose image is dense is an epimorphism. This paper considers the corresponding notion for V-functors, as identified by Lawvere. I call such functors Cauchy dense. I review the result that Cauchy dense V-functors are the cofully faithful 1-cells of V-Cat. I then show that the Cauchy completion of a V-category A is the largest V-category admitting a fully faithful Cauchy dense functor from A. I also study Cauchy dense functors in special cases, such as between monoids in V.
