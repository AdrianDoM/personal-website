I used to be a PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, under the supervision of [Tom Leinster](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~tl/). Now, I am looking for a job (either in industry or academia). I am interested in category theory and its ability to clarify relationships in many areas of mathematics.

The work I have done so far can be organised into three groups: monad theory, especially codensity and pushforward monads; enriched category theory (Cauchy dense functors); and magnitude homology, which is a homology theory for enriched categories, and in particular for metric spaces.
