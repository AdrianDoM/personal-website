I recently finished my **PhD** at the University of Edinburgh, under the supervision of [Tom Leinster](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~tl/). Now, I am **looking for a job** (either in industry or academia).

The research I have done so far can be organised into three groups: **monad theory**, especially codensity and pushforward monads; **enriched category theory** (Cauchy dense functors); and **magnitude homology**, which is a homology theory for enriched categories, and in particular for metric spaces. 

I have also worked on various mathematical formalisations using the theorem provers **Lean** and **Isabelle/HOL** (see the Coding section of [Misc](./misc/)).
