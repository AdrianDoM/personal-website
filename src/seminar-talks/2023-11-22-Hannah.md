---
title: 'Bridgeland stability conditions and group actions'
speaker: 'Hannah Dell'
---
A Bridgeland stability condition on a triangulated category \\(D\\) gives you a way to build your category out of simpler “stable” objects. Different choices of which objects are your building blocks give rise to different stability conditions. It turns out that the space of all stability conditions on \\(D\\), \\(\mathrm{Stab}(D)\\), has the structure of a complex manifold, giving us a way to extract geometry from our category. In this talk, I will introduce stability conditions, and explain a way to relate actions of finite abelian groups on \\(D\\) to the geometry of \\(\mathrm{Stab}(D)\\). Time permitting, I will discuss joint work with Edmund Heng and Antony Licata to use actions of fusion categories to generalise this to non-abelian groups.
