---
title: 'Unitary, inner product, and dagger categories'
speaker: 'Priyaa Varshinee Srinivasan'
---
A dagger category is a category equipped with a contravariant involution: it is a foundational construct in categorical quantum mechanics. The involution (dagger) is strict in the sense of being the identity-on-objects. However, there exist models with non-strict involution.

In this talk, I will present an alternative formulation of dagger categories as ‘inner product’ categories with a non-strict involution. This requires an intermediate step of considering unitary categories, a weaker version of a dagger category. Dagger, unitary, and inner product categories have a common structure which is an ‘achiral involution’ which is used to prove a three-way equivalence between the 2-categories of dagger categories, unitary categories, and inner product categories.

This work [1] is motivated by Paul-André Melliès’ work on chirality [2].

Pre-requisites: Basic category theory (definitions of functors, natural transformations, and 2-categories).

References:

[1]. Robin Cockett, Durgesh Kumar, and Priyaa Varshinee Srinivasan. "Unitary, Inner product, and Dagger categories." arXiv preprint arXiv:2603.27614 (2026).

[2]. Paul-André Melliès. Dialogue categories and chiralities. Publications of the Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences, 52:359–412, 2016.
