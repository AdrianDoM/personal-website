---
title: 'Contractads — algebraic structures underlying Graphical Configuration Spaces'
speaker: 'Anton Khoroshkin'
---
The "graphical configuration space" on a space \\(X\\) consists of collections of points indexed by the vertices of a simple graph \\(G\\), with the condition that points corresponding to adjacent vertices must be distinct. When \\(X = \mathbb{R}^d\\), the union of these spaces over all possible graphs forms an algebraic structure that we call the "little discs contractad", which extends the classical operad of little discs.

In this talk, I will provide a short overview of the theory of "contractads", focusing on the little discs contractad.
I will also present various other basic examples of contractads and discuss some applications in enumerative combinatorics.

The talk is based on joint work with Denis Lyskov: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05909](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05909), [https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21255).
