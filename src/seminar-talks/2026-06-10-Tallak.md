---
title: 'Categorification via truncated constant complete Segal objects'
speaker: 'Tallak Manum'
---
There is a well established description of \\((\infty,n)\\)-categories as iterated complete Segal objects. In this talk I will first give a short introduction to this description of \\((\infty,n)\\)-categories. I will then move on to show how a slight change of approach greatly simplifies the process of generalising results from the \\((\infty,2)\\)-category of \\((\infty,1)\\)-categories to the \\((\infty,2)\\)-category of \\((\infty,n)\\)-categories. If time permits I will give an elementary proof of straightening for \\((\infty,n)\\)-categories over a \\((\infty,1)\\)-categorical base.

This talk is based on upcoming work, but is only a slight generalisation of work by Max Blans and Thomas Bloom on \\((\infty,2)\\)-categories in appendix A of "On the chain rule in goodwillie
calculus".
