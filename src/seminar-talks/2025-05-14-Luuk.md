---
title: 'Building dagger categories via Hermitian forms'
speaker: 'Luuk Stehouwer'
note: '(Room&nbsp;5.45)'
---
Dagger categories – categories equipped with a contravariant involutive functor behaving like the Hermitian transpose – play a central role in categorical quantum mechanics and linear logic. In this talk, I will present a general construction that turns any 'anti-involutive' category into a dagger category. The key idea is to equip objects of your category with a notion of Hermitian form, inspired by finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. This construction defines a 2-functor from anti-involutive categories to dagger categories, which is left adjoint to the forgetful inclusion. In fact, this adjunction induces an equivalence between the 2-category of dagger categories and anti-involutive categories equipped with a natural extra structure. This framework, developed in joint work with Jan Steinebrunner, underpins our broader program of defining higher dagger categories.
