---
title: 'Semi-factorisation systems'
speaker: 'Malthe Sporring'
---
A strict factorisation system on a category \\(\mathcal{C}\\) is the data of two classes of morphisms (left and right) such that every morphism has a unique factorisation as a left map followed by a right map. In a semi-factorisation system, we instead have an initial such factorisation. I will describe work in progress with Tallak Manum (NTNU) to study these structures and their application to the theory of twofold monoidal categories.
