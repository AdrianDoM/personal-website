---
title: 'Equivariantization and descent for triangulated categories via monad theory'
speaker: 'Alexey Elagin'
---
I will review basic concepts of monad theory, focusing on comparison theorem. Then I will explain how this tool from abstract category theory can be used for more practical purposes and deduce some natural constructions for abelian and triangulated categories. Specifically, I plan to discuss
- relation between equivariant and derived categories,
- cohomological descent for a morphism of schemes, and
- scalar extension for linear categories.

I hope that the talk will be as elementary as possible. Besides of folklore knowledge, it is based on papers “Cohomological descent theory for a morphism of stacks and for equivariant derived categories”, [arXiv:1103.3135](https://arxiv.org/abs/1103.3135) and “On equivariant triangulated categories”, [arXiv:1403.7027](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7027).
