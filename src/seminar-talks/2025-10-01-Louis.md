---
title: 'One rig to rule them all'
speaker: 'Louis Lemonnier'
---
A rig category is a category equipped with two monoidal structures, one
distributing over the other. Think of sets and bijections under product
and disjoint union, or vector spaces under tensor products and direct
sums, or Hilbert spaces and unitary maps with the same operations. Rig
structure plays a key role in the study of reversible and quantum
programming. We discuss the computational role of the rig structure.
Starting from a prop generating a 'base circuit theory', we universally
extend it to a rig category. We then show that the newly obtained
circuit theory is governed by a handful of equations over
(multi-)controlled gates. Intuitively, this shows that rig structure can
be regarded as controlled monoidal structure. More precisely, this
isolates computational control aspects from circuits, which is borne out
in many examples, for both Boolean circuits and quantum circuits.
