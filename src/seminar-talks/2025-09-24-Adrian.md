---
title: 'Cauchy completeness and density'
speaker: 'Adrián Doña Mateo'
---
In 1973, Lawvere pointed out that metric spaces are particular examples of categories enriched in the poset \\(([0,\infty], \geq)\\). Not only that, but he identified complete metric spaces categorically in a very elegant way: they are exactly those \\([0,\infty]\\)-categories which have all absolute (weighted) colimits. This gives the notion of Cauchy complete \\(\mathcal{V}\\)-category, which turns out to be closely linked to the theory of Morita equivalence. I will give an overview of this story and of the type of functors associated to it: the Cauchy dense functors.
