---
title: "Just Kan't Get Enough"
speaker: 'Jack Heaney'
---
An intuitive feeling for understanding statistics and probability is the formula: Statistical Inference = Probability\\(^{-1}\\). Is it possible to translate this intuition into serious mathematics? That is the question that Pugh et al. ask and answer through the theory
of Kan extensions. We will unpack how to reinterpret the above formula in a 2-categorical framework by considering the categorification of error minimisation schemes. By choosing a specific context in which to interpret this error (a monoidal preorder), we
can leverage this rich theory to guarantee the existence of a global minimisation algorithm. This has potential applications for algorithms in machine learning.
