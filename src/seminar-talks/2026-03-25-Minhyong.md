---
title: 'Diophantine geometry via Tannakian Categories'
speaker: 'Minhyong Kim'
---
Diophantine geometry studies rational or integral solutions to polynomial equations mostly using the techniques of algebraic geometry generalised to base rings like the integers, a framework also known as 'arithmetic geometry'. In recent years, somewhat more difficult ideas from arithmetic homotopy theory have been employed successfully to the computation of points and come to be known as the 'nonabelian method of Chabauty' (also called Chabauty-Kim theory by very kind people). At the core of the techniques are suitable Tannakian categories and fiber functors defined on them via rational points on algebraic varieties. In this talk, I will give a casual introduction to this part of the theory.
