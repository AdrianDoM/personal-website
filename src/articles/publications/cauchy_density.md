---
title: Cauchy density
link: 'https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.07869'
linkType: arxiv
journal: 'arXiv preprint'
date: 2025-06-01
---
A continuous map of metric spaces whose image is dense is an epimorphism. This
paper considers the corresponding notion for _V_-functors, as identified by
Lawvere. I call such functors Cauchy dense. I review the result that Cauchy
dense _V_-functors are the cofully faithful 1-cells of _V_-**Cat**. I then show
that the Cauchy completion of a _V_-category _A_ is the largest _V_-category
admitting a fully faithful Cauchy dense functor from _A_. I also study Cauchy
dense functors in special cases, such as between monoids in _V_.
