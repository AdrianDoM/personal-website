My master's thesis, supervised by Jacob Rasmussen.
It builds up to the construction of HOMFLY-PT homology of oriented links, which
itself categorifies the HOMFLY-PT polynomial. In doing so it introduces, the 
category of Soergel bimodules, which is a categorification of the Hecke algebra.
It sketches a proof of the invariance of HOMFLY-PT homology under link isotopy,
and carefully computes the homology of the (2,_n_)-torus link.
